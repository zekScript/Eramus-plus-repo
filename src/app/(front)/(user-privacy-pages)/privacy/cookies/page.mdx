## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the website owners.

Cookies serve various purposes, such as enabling core functionality, tracking user behavior to improve experience, remembering preferences, and enhancing security.

---

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use several types of cookies for different purposes. Some are set by us, while others may be set by third-party providers whose services we use.

### 2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of the Website. Without them, services such as secure login or remembering privacy preferences cannot be provided.

- **Examples**: Session authentication, CSRF token, security configuration.

### 2.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us collect data on how users interact with our Website, which pages are visited most often, and whether users experience any errors. This information is used to improve the Website’s performance.

- **Examples**: Google Analytics, page view counters, click tracking.

### 2.3 Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow the Website to remember choices you make (such as your username, language, or region) and provide enhanced, more personalized features.

- **Examples**: Remembering dark/light mode, language settings, recently viewed items.

### 2.4 Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services on our Website (e.g., embedded videos, analytics, or social sharing buttons), which may set cookies in your browser. These cookies are governed by the respective third parties’ privacy policies.

- **Examples**: YouTube embedded video player, Google Fonts, GitHub Gist embeds.

---

## 3. Cookie Duration

Cookies can be either:

- **Session Cookies**: Deleted when you close your browser.
- **Persistent Cookies**: Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted.

## 4. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

- Ensure the Website operates properly and securely
- Track site performance and identify bugs or issues
- Analyze traffic and usage patterns to improve content and layout
- Store your preferences (language, layout, etc.)
- Authenticate users and prevent fraudulent activity

We do **not** use cookies for advertising or retargeting purposes.

## 5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options to control or restrict the use of cookies:

### 5.1 Through Browser Settings

Most modern browsers allow you to:

- View and delete stored cookies
- Block cookies by default
- Block third-party cookies
- Set site-specific cookie permissions

You can refer to your browser’s help documentation for exact instructions.

- [Chrome Settings](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox Settings](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences)
- [Safari Settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201265)
- [Edge Settings](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/4468242)

### 5.2 Opting Out of Analytics

To opt out of Google Analytics, you can use the official browser add-on:  
[https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

### 5.3 Blocking or Deleting Cookies

Keep in mind that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Website. Certain features, such as logging in or remembering your preferences, may not work as intended.
